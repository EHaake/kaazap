# Plan: Series-aware banter, spoken word by word — spec 030

**Status**: Final — signed off 2026-09-23 (one review, one re-review)
**Implements**: `spec.md` in this directory

## Context

Three things change, all at the `App` layer and in drawing; the engine never
learns any of them.

1. **Which line is said.** `banter.rs` gains six line pools per voice (Leading,
   Trailing, All square, Decider, series won, series lost) and two pure pool
   choosers. `App` asks them with the series the board already reads at match
   start, with the locked series at the venue, and with "did this match decide
   a series" at match end.
2. **How a line is said.** The shown line becomes a `Speech` — the line plus
   how much of it has been said — advanced in `tick` like spec 027's
   `BoardMotion`. Each newly shown word plays one `Sfx::Burble`, generated by
   `scripts/gen_sfx.py`.
3. **Two places that show new things.** The venue's presence panel grows two
   rows to hold the venue line (the same interior row the board uses), and the
   deciding match's game-over frame keeps the final series score through a
   one-shot `App` field.

No engine file, no `campaign.rs`, `profile.rs`, `save.rs`, `economy.rs` or
`opponent.rs` change; no persisted field; no crate. `SAVE_VERSION` and
`PROFILE_VERSION` stay 1.

The places this is most likely to go wrong, and where the design went:

- **Stray burbles** — a burble from a line that was replaced, cleared, or left
  behind on another screen. §Design tensions 1 and 3.
- **Arrival versus return at the venue** — the venue is rebuilt by the same
  function on both. §Design tension 6.
- **The deciding match** — settlement clears the live series one tick before
  the game-over frame and the closing line both need it. §Design tension 8.
- **"Never louder than the music"** — a sentence until it is a number.
  §Design tension 9.

## What the code already gives us

- **Banter is an `App` observer.** `update_banter` diffs `BanterSnapshot`s;
  `start_match` seeds the greeting; the Continue arm blanks the line (spec 017:
  "no greeting for a match already underway"). The shown line is
  `App::banter: Option<&'static str>`; `banter_last` feeds `pick`'s
  no-repeat rule and survives the phase-clear. All line pools are
  `&'static [&'static str]` on a per-voice `BanterSet` const.
- **The line is drawn centred by character count.** `draw_presence_extras`
  draws it on the panel's interior row 14 through `draw_text_in(…,
  Align::Center, …)`, whose x is `rect.x0 + (rect_w - len) / 2` with `len` the
  clipped string's `chars().count()`. So a string the same length as the
  finished line, with the unsaid words blanked to spaces, draws every said word
  at exactly its finished column. `BoardView::draw` already takes
  `banter: Option<&str>`, so its signature does not change.
- **The compact board draws no line at all** (spec 026: below 139 columns, no
  panel, no banter). The venue draws its presence panel at every width.
- **Spec 027's motion idiom**: clocks advanced in `tick` by `dt`, pure
  functions of elapsed time (`thinking_suffix_at`), beats as named constants in
  `lib.rs` with a bounds test in the module's tests, and state discarded in
  `tick`'s `match &self.screen` the moment its screen is not up. The spoken
  line reuses all four.
- **Audio**: each `Sfx` is an embedded WAV from `scripts/gen_sfx.py`;
  `Audio::play_cue(Cue { sfx, pitch })` plays one at a pitch (`speed`), and
  `AudioState::play` gates every SFX on `should_play_sfx` (mute, zero volume)
  and scales it by `amplify(sfx_volume)`. Music plays through
  `Player::set_volume(music_volume)`. Both scalings are linear, so "at equal
  settings" compares the two assets directly. The defaults are music 0.5, SFX
  0.8. The script regenerates all thirteen files on every run, with
  `random.seed(0)` for `bust`'s noise.
- **One function sets the campaign screen.** `open_campaign_home` builds a
  fresh `VenueState::new()` (only a cursor) or the map. Its callers, by `grep
  -n "open_campaign_home()" src/app.rs`: `enter_campaign` (every menu entry
  and the game-over acknowledgement), `launch_from_map` (starting a series),
  the Card Shop's Back arm, the deck builder's `BackTo::Campaign` arm, and one
  test (`app.open_campaign_home()`, ~3246). The first two are arrivals; the two
  Back arms are returns.
- **Settlement happens on the `GameOver` edge in `tick`**, through
  `Profile::resolve_match`, whose `Settlement.series` is the `SeriesOutcome`.
  `record_series_match` sets the live series to `None` when it decides it. The
  in-flight pointer stays set until the acknowledgement, so
  `board_series_line(in_progress, series)` matches node and series throughout
  the game-over frame — it just has no series left to show.
- **The `victory_due` idiom, checked against the code** (DECISIONS, spec 029,
  *Asked, and not answered*): a plain `App` field set from the `Settlement` in
  `tick`'s resolution block and `mem::take`n in `enter_campaign`, never saved.
  Accurate. The held score here is a sibling field set at the same point, but
  discarded the motion way (§Design tension 8).
- **The venue presence panel is `VENUE_PANEL_H` = 15 rows** (border, name,
  portrait) top-aligned beside the 22-row art box; the rows below it are blank
  on purpose. It has no line row.
- **`App` tests read the real profile and settings.** `App::new` loads
  `Settings`, `Profile` and runs the save check, and `Profile::save()` has no
  `cfg(test)` guard (ROADMAP, path-seam item). **No new test constructs an
  `App`**; every decision this spec makes sits behind a pure function.

## Design tensions resolved

### 1. One line, one clock: `Speech` replaces `banter`

The reveal state lives with the line, in one `Option<Speech>` that replaces
`App::banter`, rather than a second field beside it. Replacing the line
replaces its clock, and clearing the line drops it, so interruption (AC 10) is
a property of the data rather than something each call site has to remember.
`banter_last` stays exactly as it is.

### 2. In place by blanking, not by slicing

`revealed(line, n)` returns the line with every character of words after the
`n`th replaced by a space, so it always has the finished line's length. Any
centring drawer then puts each said word where it will sit in the finished
line (AC 8), with no new geometry. The alternative — pass the drawer an offset
and a prefix — would duplicate `draw_text_in`'s centring arithmetic in a second
place.

### 3. Burbles come only from advancing the one `Speech`, never queued

`Speech::advance(dt) -> bool` says whether a new word appeared on this step;
`App` plays at most one burble per step, and plays the first word's burble when
the line is said. Nothing is scheduled in the audio thread, so a replaced or
cleared line cannot burble again: there is nothing left to advance.

**Burbles never overlap**, because two at once play louder, which is the one
thing the spec forbids. Every burble goes through one `App::burble(word)`,
which plays only if `audio::burble_clear(since_burble)` holds: at least
`BURBLE_GAP_MS` (150) since the last one. Two bounds make that safe, and a
test pins both. The burble, at its slowest per-word pitch, ends within
`BURBLE_GAP_MS`, so a burble that is allowed never overlaps the one before.
And `BURBLE_GAP_MS <= 3 * GAME_LOOP_SLEEP_MS`: every tick is at least
`GAME_LOOP_SLEEP_MS` (sleep plus the loop's work, `main.rs`), so three ticks
cover the gap, and while ticks stay under `WORD_STEP_MS / 3` (≈ 66 ms) two
consecutive words of one line are at least three ticks apart — the guard never
drops a word of an ordinary line. (Corrected at the sign-off re-review: the
earlier `<= WORD_STEP_MS − GAME_LOOP_SLEEP_MS` reasoning assumed ticks are *at
most* 50 ms; they are at least that. The numbers are unchanged.) The guard drops a burble in exactly two cases, both
divergences from "one burble per word" that exist to keep the sound soft:

- **An interruption within 150 ms of the old line's last burble.**
  `advance_speech` runs before `update_banter` in `tick`, so an old word and a
  new line's first word can fall on the same step or one step apart. The new
  line's first word then appears silently, and its later words burble as usual.
- **A stalled frame** that crosses two word boundaries shows both words and
  owes one burble.

### 4. The Animations setting is read when the line is said, not when it is drawn

Spec 027 passes `settings.animations.then_some(&motion)` at draw time. A spoken
line cannot: the setting decides how many burbles it is owed, and that is
settled when the line is chosen. `Speech::new(line, animated)` starts with every
word said when `animated` is false (AC 12: whole, one burble). The setting can
only change from the Settings overlay over the start menu, where no line is on
screen, so reading it at say-time is never stale.

### 5. A burble plays only for a word the player can see — ruled 7A

The spec says each word "as it appears, plays one … burble." On the compact
board (89–138 columns) no line appears at all, so no burble plays there —
**the person's ruling 7A (2026-09-23)**. At the venue the panel draws at every
width, so venue lines always burble. The rule is one predicate,
`App::line_visible()`, which reads the board's existing `BoardView::is_wide()`
(`board.rs`: `self.layout.opponent_panel.is_some()`), so `board.rs` does not
change.

### 6. Arrival is the caller's word, not something remembered

`open_campaign_home` keeps its single job and stays the only place either
campaign screen is set (spec 029's invariant grep still returns exactly two
lines). A new `arrive_at_campaign()` calls it and then, if the screen is the
venue, has the opponent say a line. The two arrival callers (`enter_campaign`,
`launch_from_map`) switch to it; the two Back arms keep calling
`open_campaign_home`, so a return says nothing new. The spec's other half of a
return — "the line already shown is still there, drawn whole, with no burble" —
comes from `tick`. Any screen other than the board or the venue **settles** the
current `Speech` (every word said, silently), the way it resets `BoardMotion`.
Coming back from the Card Shop draws that settled line. Rejected: a flag on
`VenueState` (it is rebuilt on every return), and an `arrival: bool` parameter
on `open_campaign_home` (every caller would have to pass the right value, and
spec 015's bug was a caller forgetting what a return is).

### 7. Where the series stands is banter's question, answered from what the board reads

`SeriesState` and `series_state(&Series)` live in `banter.rs`, not
`campaign.rs`. It is a from-the-opponent's-side reading used only to pick
lines, and `campaign.rs` stays untouched. At match start the series comes from
`match_series(in_progress, series)`, which is `board_series_line`'s existing
node-matches-series rule pulled out into its own function. That makes
Quick Play, a rematch, and a match left in flight with no series all read as
"no series", the same way the board does. At the venue it comes from the
locked series itself, because the pointer has already been cleared by then.
The venue and the match that follows it read the same state (nothing settles
between them), so they draw from one pool. `pick` is fed `banter_last`, so the
match never opens on the venue's line (AC 6).

**`start_match` feeds `banter_last` to `pick` only inside a series**:
`let state = self.series_state_now(); let last = state.and(self.banter_last);`.
A series match start therefore avoids the line the venue just said (AC 6).
Every match with no series — Quick Play, a cleared-planet rematch, a match left
in flight without one — passes `None` exactly as `start_match` does today
(spec 017's no-repeat rule is within a match). AC 4 holds literally, for the
pool and for `pick`'s input. The venue arrival always has a series, so it
always passes `banter_last`.

### 8. The deciding match: one held value serves both the closing line and the frame

`App::final_series: Option<Series>` is the decided series at its final score.
It is set in `tick`'s resolution block, from the match's series cloned before
`resolve_match` plus this match's result, and only when the outcome is `Won` or
`Lost`. `update_banter` runs later in the same tick and sees it: a `MatchWin` /
`MatchLoss` with `final_series` set draws from series won / series lost. The
`InGame` draw arm passes `campaign.series().or(self.final_series.as_ref())` to
`board_series_line`, so the game-over frame shows the final score (AC 14).
The map never reads the field. `tick` discards it on any screen but the board,
beside the `BoardMotion` reset. That differs from `victory_due`'s take-on-entry
because the held score belongs to one screen: this way it is gone on every exit
from the match, not just the one exit that exists today.

The final score is computed by `decided_series(before, outcome, player_won)`,
which adds one to the winner's tally. That repeats `record_series_match`'s
increment instead of changing `SeriesOutcome` to carry the score, which would
touch `campaign.rs`, `profile.rs` and every `MapBanner` match. A test drives
`CampaignRun::record_series_match` through series of both lengths and checks
that `decided_series` agrees with it at every decision, so the repetition
cannot drift.

### 9. "Never louder than the music", as a number

**The burble's peak sample amplitude is at most the music track's RMS
amplitude times `default music volume / default SFX volume`** (0.5 / 0.8 =
0.625), with the music's RMS measured over its first 60 seconds. Since both
volumes scale linearly, this means:

- at **equal** settings (the spec's condition) the burble's loudest instant is
  under two-thirds of the music's average level, which is clearly softer;
- at the **default** settings the player starts on, where SFX is 1.6× the
  music, the burble's loudest instant still does not exceed the music's average.

A unit test in `audio.rs` decodes both embedded assets with rodio's `Decoder`
(no audio device is opened) and asserts this, reading the defaults from
`Settings::default()` so a change of default is caught. The first 60 seconds
bounds the test's decode time in a debug build. If the track opens quietly,
that only makes the bound stricter. The person's ear is the other half (AC 9).
If they ask for a burble louder than this ceiling allows, that is a conflict
with the spec's own sentence and goes back to them (§Open questions 3).

A tweak by ear is a **parameter change**: every number that shapes the sound
sits in one `BURBLE` block in `gen_sfx.py` (§Design 3), plus the per-word pitch
table in `audio.rs`. `python3 scripts/gen_sfx.py burble` regenerates that one
file and leaves the other thirteen byte-identical.

### 10. The venue line goes inside the presence panel, which grows two rows

`VENUE_PANEL_H` goes from 15 to 17 (border, name, portrait, a gap row, the line
row, border), so the venue line sits on the panel's interior row 14, exactly
where the board draws it. One drawer, `portrait::draw_banter_line`, is taken out
of `draw_presence_extras` for both callers (the second caller is what earns it).
At every size the panel still ends above the art box's bottom (21 ≤ 26 at the
fit sizes; the offsets are constant). Rejected: a line floating under the
15-row panel, which needs no layout change but reads as a caption *under* the
opponent rather than the opponent speaking, and differs from the board.
**Cost, stated**: six pinned portrait `Rect`s and one area comment in
`layout.rs`'s venue tests move — a named class in T007, and a deviation from
AC 11's parenthetical "existing tests untouched". That parenthetical is about
phases, popups and timings, none of which these assertions pin.

### 11. A resumed match has no line, so it has nothing to say — ruled 8A

Today the Continue arm blanks the line on purpose (spec 017), and the next event
supplies one. **The person's ruling 8A (2026-09-23)** keeps that: a resumed
match stays blank, and no line is spoken, which is what `spec.md`'s AC 13 now
says. The Continue arm sets `self.speech = None`. The next event's line is a new
line and is spoken like any other.

### 12. Scale

Two new types (`Speech`, `SeriesState`), one new sound, three small `App`
methods (`say`, `advance_speech`, `burble`), one shared drawer. No trait, no per-opponent
burble table (a non-goal), no reveal configuration beyond one constant. The six
pools are plain fields on the existing `BanterSet` rather than a nested
`SeriesLines` struct. There are eleven voices and one consumer, so a second
type would only rename the fields.

### 2. `src/banter.rs`

**The reveal** (pure, beside `pick`):

```rust
/// The number of words in `line`: its maximal runs of non-space characters.
pub fn word_count(line: &str) -> usize

/// `line` with only its first `words` words showing — each later word's
/// characters blanked to spaces — so the text always has the finished line's
/// length and every shown word sits where it will in the finished line,
/// whatever centres it (spec 030, AC 8). `words >= word_count` is the line.
pub fn revealed(line: &str, words: usize) -> String

/// Words showing `elapsed` after the line was chosen: the first at once, one
/// more each WORD_STEP_MS, never more than the line has.
pub fn words_due(line: &str, elapsed: Duration) -> usize

/// A line being spoken (spec 030): the line, the time since it was chosen, and
/// how many of its words are showing. Drawing state only — never saved.
#[derive(Debug, Clone, PartialEq, Eq)]
pub struct Speech { line: &'static str, elapsed: Duration, shown: usize }

impl Speech {
    /// Just chosen: its first word showing, or — `animated` false, the
    /// Animations setting Off — every word at once. The caller plays the one
    /// burble the first appearance owes.
    pub fn new(line: &'static str, animated: bool) -> Self
    /// Advance by `dt`. True when a word appeared on this step, meaning the caller
    /// owes one burble. A step that crosses more than one word boundary shows
    /// them all and still returns true once (plan §Design tension 3).
    /// `shown` never decreases, so a settled speech stays settled.
    pub fn advance(&mut self, dt: Duration) -> bool
    /// Show the rest at once, silently — the line's screen was left.
    pub fn settle(&mut self)
    pub fn words_shown(&self) -> usize
    /// What the panel draws: `revealed(line, shown)`.
    pub fn text(&self) -> String
}
```

`lines_for` is unchanged. The module doc gains one paragraph naming spec 030's
series pools and `Speech`.

**The lines.** 11 voices × (3 Leading + 3 Trailing + 3 Decider + 2 series won
+ 2 series lost), plus 3 All square for The Sovereign — 146 new lines. Each is
written in the voice the const's doc comment describes (Greeb's jittery
surprise, Dax's excuses, the Magistrate's legal diction, the Sovereign's glacial
minimum, the fallback's neutral plainness). From the opponent's side: Leading
gloats or presses, Trailing needles or bristles, Decider meets the last match
as the last match, series won/lost speaks of the whole series rather than the
match. Rules, all but the last enforced by tests: at most `BANTER_MAX_WIDTH`
(20) characters; at most 5 words (finishes inside a second at 200 ms a word);
no duplicate within a pool; no line shared with any other voice, including the
existing lines; ASCII apart from what the existing lines already use. Not
enforced: prefer not to repeat a line from the same voice's other pools. **No
existing line changes.** The person edits the new ones at the Phase 2 pause.

### 3. `scripts/gen_sfx.py` + `assets/sfx/burble.wav` (new)

A `burble()` synth and one parameter block at module level, with a comment
naming spec 030 and the tweak workflow:

```python
# The opponent's burble (spec 030): one soft, voice-like murmur per spoken word.
# Every number that shapes it is here, so a tweak by ear is an edit to this
# block and `python3 scripts/gen_sfx.py burble`. `peak` is bounded by the audio
# test `the_burble_is_softer_than_the_music`; `length_s` bounded by BURBLE_GAP_MS.
BURBLE = {
    "length_s": 0.12,          # within BURBLE_GAP_MS even at the slowest pitch
    "pitch_hz": 150,           # the voice's fundamental
    "glide": -0.12,            # fractional pitch fall over the burble
    "vibrato_hz": 18, "vibrato_depth": 0.04,   # the murmur's wobble in pitch …
    "tremolo_hz": 24, "tremolo_depth": 0.35,   # … and in level
    "formants": ((500, 750, 90), (1100, 1400, 140)),  # (start Hz, end Hz, width Hz)
    "harmonics": 18,           # fewer = rounder, more = buzzier
    "attack_s": 0.015, "release_s": 0.06,
    "peak": 0.10,              # loudest sample, full scale 1.0 — set from the test's measurement
}
```

The technique is **additive**. It sums `harmonics` harmonics of a gliding,
vibrato'd fundamental. Each harmonic's amplitude is `1/k` times the summed
resonance of two formants whose centres glide from start to end (a "wo→a"
vowel). The result gets a raised-cosine attack and release and a tremolo, and
is normalised so its largest sample is `peak`. It uses no `random`, so it
cannot shift `bust`'s seeded noise. The values above are a starting point. The
implementer picks `peak` against the music RMS the test prints, comfortably
under the ceiling, and records the number. `main()` gains optional names:
`python3 scripts/gen_sfx.py burble` writes only the named sounds, and no
arguments writes all of them, as today. Output is mono 16-bit 44.1 kHz through
the existing `write_wav`.

### 5. `src/app.rs`

**Phase 1 — the speech seam (T003).**

- Field `banter: Option<&'static str>` → `speech: Option<Speech>` (doc: "The
  opponent's line being spoken (spec 030; the line was spec 017's `banter`).
  None when no line shows."). `App::new`: `speech: None`.
- ```rust
  /// Say `line` (spec 030): it replaces any line still being spoken, at once,
  /// from its first word, and becomes `banter_last`; its first word's burble
  /// plays now — if the line is on screen. With Animations off it is whole at
  /// once, and this is its only burble.
  fn say(&mut self, line: &'static str)

  /// Whether the line is drawn (plan §Design tension 5, ruling 7A): at the
  /// venue always, on the board only when `board_view.is_wide()`, never under
  /// the too-small screen.
  fn line_visible(&self) -> bool

  /// Speak the current line on: while its screen — the board or the venue —
  /// is up, one more word every WORD_STEP_MS, each with its burble; on any
  /// other screen it settles, whole and silent, so returning shows it without
  /// a sound (spec 030).
  fn advance_speech(&mut self, dt: Duration)

  /// Play word `word`'s burble — unless one played less than BURBLE_GAP_MS
  /// ago, so two never overlap (plan §Design tension 3). The only place a
  /// burble is sent.
  fn burble(&mut self, word: usize)
  ```
  A field `since_burble: Duration` (doc: "time since the last burble (spec
  030); starts at `Duration::MAX` so the first is always clear"), advanced
  with `saturating_add(dt)` at the top of `advance_speech`, is reset to zero
  by `burble` when it plays. `advance_speech` computes `line_visible()` before
  borrowing `self.speech`, then calls `self.burble(words_shown - 1)` when
  `advance` returns true and the line is visible. `say` calls `self.burble(0)`
  when visible. **`burble_cue(` is called in exactly one place** (`burble`),
  and `self.burble(` in exactly two (`say`, `advance_speech`).
- `tick`: call `self.advance_speech(dt)` just before `self.emit_audio_cues()`,
  so a line said later in this tick keeps its full first step.

**`banter.rs` (T001)**
