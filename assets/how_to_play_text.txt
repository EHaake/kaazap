=======  How to Play  =======

Beat the opponent's total without passing
20. First to 3 round wins takes the match.

Draw a card (0-10) each turn; play side
cards to adjust, then Stand. Your table
holds 12 cards; filling it stands you.

 +N / -N   add or subtract N
 ±N        pick + or - when played
 2&4 / 3&6 flip matching cards' signs
 ±1T       tiebreaker (±1), wins a tie

Over 20 isn't bust - recover with a minus
card, or press d/s to bust.

Campaign: every match is staked. Win and it
comes back doubled; lose and it's gone. Go
broke and the run resets. The Card Shop sells
cards; the ante rises as you go deeper.
Each opponent is Best of 3 matches, the last
Best of 5. A started series is played out.

1-4 or ←/→ pick a card, Enter plays it,
Space draws, S stands. ? closes.
