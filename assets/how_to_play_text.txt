=======  How to Play  =======

Beat the opponent's total without passing
20. First to 3 round wins takes the match.

Draw a card (0-10) each turn; play side
cards to adjust, then Stand.

 +N / -N   add or subtract N
 ±N        pick + or - when played
 2&4 / 3&6 flip matching cards' signs
 ±1T       tiebreaker (±1), wins a tie

Over 20 isn't bust - recover with a minus
card, or press d/s to bust.

Play with 1-4 or arrows + Enter.  ? closes.
