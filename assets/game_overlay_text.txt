=== Controls ===

  1 2 3 4 / ← →  Select a hand card
  ↑ / ↓          Flip a ± card's sign
  Enter / P      Play the selected card
  Space / D      Draw a card (ends turn)
  S              Stand (ends turn)
  N / Space      Next round
  G / Space      New game
  Esc / X        Back to the menu
  Q              Quit
  ?              Close this overlay

  Ctrl-P/N/B/F mirror the arrow keys.
  Over 20: Space, D or S accepts the bust.
