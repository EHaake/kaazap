=== Controls ===

  ← / →      Select a hand card
  ↑ / ↓      Flip a ± card's sign
  Enter      Play the selected card
  1 2 3 4    Play a hand card
  D / Space  Draw a card (ends turn)
  S          Stand (ends turn)
  N / Space  Next round
  G / Space  New game
  Esc / X    Back to the menu
  Q          Quit
  ?          Close this overlay

  Ctrl-P/N/B/F mirror the arrow keys.
  Over 20: D or S accepts the bust.
