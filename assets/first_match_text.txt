=====  How a match works  =====

Draw toward 20 without going over.
Stand to lock in your total.
Play a side card from your hand of four to adjust it.
First to 3 rounds wins the match.

 Space  draw          S  stand
 1-4 or ←/→  pick a card    Enter  play it
 ↑/↓  flip a ± card's sign

             Enter to begin
